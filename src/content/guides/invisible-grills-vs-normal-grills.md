## What is the main difference?

A normal grill is a frame of solid iron or steel bars, or a mesh, fixed across the opening. An invisible grill is a set of thin stainless steel wires, fixed close together and pulled tight between a top and bottom channel. Both stop people and pets from falling out. They differ in how much of the view they take away and how much care they need.

## How do they compare?

**View.** Invisible grill wires are thin enough that most people stop noticing them. Solid bars are always part of the picture, which matters most on high floors with a good view.

**Upkeep.** Stainless steel wire is corrosion-resistant and low maintenance. Ordinary iron grills usually need repainting to stay in good condition, especially on exposed balconies.

**Look.** Invisible grills keep the lines of the building clean. Normal grills read as a visible railing or cage.

**Fitting.** An invisible grill depends on correct measurement and tension. Wires that are too loose or spaced too far apart do not protect properly, so the fitting matters as much as the product. Normal grills are more forgiving to fit.

**Cost.** Prices depend on the area, the wire spacing or bar design, and how easy the balcony is to reach, so compare quotes for the same size and the same material. [Ask for a quote](/invisible-grill-price/) rather than comparing prices by rule of thumb.

## When does a normal grill still make sense?

If the view does not matter to you, or you want a very low-cost option for a small opening, a normal grill can be enough. If you only need to stop birds, a net may be a better fit than either grill.

## Which should you choose?

Choose an invisible grill when the view, a clean look and low upkeep matter and you are happy to have it fitted by a team that measures properly.
