## Can invisible grills keep children safe?

They can. A properly fitted invisible grill closes the opening with tightly spaced, tensioned stainless steel wires, so a child cannot slip through or fall out of the balcony. That only holds when the grill is fitted correctly. This is general guidance, not a guarantee, and it does not replace watching young children.

## What should you check?

**Wire spacing.** The gaps between wires must be too narrow for a child to pass. Ask what spacing is being used for your balcony and tell the installer how old your child is.

**Tension.** Wires should be pulled tight along their full length. Loose wires can be pushed apart.

**Fixing.** The top and bottom channels must be firmly fixed to a solid frame. Ask how and where they will be fixed.

**Coverage.** Check there is no gap at the corners, at the sides, or around pipes and railings.

**Finish.** Stainless steel wire is corrosion-resistant, which helps it stay in good condition over time on exposed balconies.

## What else helps at home?

Keep chairs, planters and boxes away from the grill so a child cannot use them as a step. Keep the balcony door secure when you are not there. Check the grill from time to time and ask for a re-tension if anything feels slack.
