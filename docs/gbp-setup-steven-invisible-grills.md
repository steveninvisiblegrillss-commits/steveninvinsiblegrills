## 0. First: new profile or rename? (decides everything)

Search Google Maps for the phone number **6305721219** and for "MRR Safety Nets", "MRR Invisible Grills".

| Situation | Do this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A profile already exists under an MRR name, owned by the client | **Rename it** to "Steven Invisible Grills" (Edit profile > Business name). Do NOT create a second profile | Keeps all existing reviews, photos and ranking history. Two profiles with the same phone are treated as duplicates and one can be suspended |
| A profile exists but someone else owns it | Request ownership via the "Own this business?" link, then rename | Same reason |
| No profile exists | Create a new one at business.google.com named "Steven Invisible Grills" | Clean start |

**Name rules (Google guidelines):**
- Business name field = exactly `Steven Invisible Grills`. Nothing else.
- Do NOT add keywords or locations ("Steven Invisible Grills & Pigeon Nets Hyderabad", "Best Invisible Grills"). Keyword-stuffed names get suspended.
- Google may ask for proof that this is the real-world name. Before renaming, the client should have at least one of: shop/office signboard, vehicle branding, printed invoice or GST certificate, visiting cards, all showing "Steven Invisible Grills". A rename often triggers re-verification.

## 5. Reviews (the biggest prominence factor you control)

1. Copy the review link: GBP dashboard > "Ask for reviews" > copy short link. Put it in the website config (`site.reviewUrl`).
2. After every installation, send this on WhatsApp (Paste):

> Hi {Name}, thank you for choosing Steven Invisible Grills for your {service} in {area}. If you're happy with the work, could you leave us a short Google review? It really helps other families find us: {review link}

3. Reply to every review within 48 hours, mention the service and area naturally ("Thanks Ravi, glad the pigeon net on your Kukatpally balcony is working well").
4. Never buy reviews, offer discounts for reviews, or ask only happy customers (review gating). These break Google policy.
5. Old reviews stay on the profile after a rename; reply to new ones using the new name.
